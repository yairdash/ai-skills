# Hebrew and editable delivery

Use direction: rtl and text-align consistently; isolate English numbers,
names and codes with bdi or dir=ltr. Do not reverse strings to fake RTL.
Verify punctuation, mixed-language lines, currency and numerals in decoded
frames. Use a licensed Hebrew-capable font and preserve its license when
bundling; the kit does not redistribute the computer's Windows fonts.

Check text at actual phone size, safe areas, subtitle line breaks and reading
time. Retain each subtitle's relation to the edited audio, not only the
original transcript. Whisper output requires human/contextual verification
for names, code-switching and ambiguous Hebrew words.

For final delivery save: brief/script, source project, asset manifest,
license notes, dimensions/fps, EDL and retimed captions when relevant, MP4,
and VERIFY.md stating actual checks and unresolved limitations.
HTML/GSAP and Remotion timelines are editable in their own tools. They do not
automatically become native CapCut or DaVinci timelines. For later editor
handoff preserve clean footage, separate audio and captions, and cut timing.
