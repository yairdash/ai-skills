# Rights and Publication

This is a workflow checklist, not legal advice.

- Use H3-native vocal resemblance only; no cloned actor audio without documented consent or license.
- Do not imply official status, endorsement, or affiliation.
- Avoid copied dialogue, catchphrases as the main joke, reconstructed scenes, and unlicensed source media.
- Record whether references are owned, licensed, generated, or found online.

For realistic synthetic likeness or voice, prepare clear AI disclosure and use current platform controls. Parody does not automatically defeat likeness, impersonation, trademark, copyright, or platform complaints.

Refresh YouTube’s synthetic-content guidance at https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 and impersonation policy at https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801947 before publication. Block false endorsement and escalate uncertain commercial questions to qualified counsel.
