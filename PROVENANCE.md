# Sources and licensing

External links point to the original project. Their code, license and availability are controlled by that project. Linking does not relicense or endorse its contents.

Before hosting any third-party skill:

1. Verify the repository, exact path, revision and applicable license, including bundled scripts/assets.
2. Preserve required copyright, LICENSE and NOTICE files.
3. Copy the complete skill folder and identify sibling/shared dependencies. Do not assume it is self-contained.
4. Record `source.repository`, `source.path`, `source.revision`, the original URL, license and any local modifications in `PROVENANCE.json` alongside the skill.
5. If permission is unclear, retain only an external link.

New original content needs an explicit license decision before redistribution is offered. No blanket license is applied to third-party content or to unpublished personal skills.
